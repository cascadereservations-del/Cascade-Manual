Guest Journey SOPs

The 5-star standard from first inquiry to post-stay review.

1. Inquiry & Booking: First Impressions

Be fast, friendly, and helpful. Make them feel welcome before they even book.

  • Do: Respond within 15 minutes. Use the "Inquiry Response" template but personalize it with their name.
  • Don't: Give one-word answers. Never ignore a question, even if the answer is in the listing.

💡 Pro Tip: If a guest seems hesitant, a message like "Let me know if you have any other questions at all, happy to help you plan your trip to GenSan! 🌿" builds trust and often secures the booking.

2. Pre-Arrival: Build Anticipation

Make check-in feel easy and secure. Give them everything they need before they have to ask.

  • Do: Send the "Pre-Arrival (T-24h)" message exactly 24 hours before check-in. This consistency is professional.
  • Don't: Send check-in details too early (security risk) or too late (causes guest anxiety).

💡 Pro Tip: Run a final QC check (Wi-Fi speed, AC, TV) *right before* sending the pre-arrival message. This prevents 90% of arrival-day issues.

3. Post-Stay: A Gracious Farewell

Make checkout simple and thank them sincerely. A great last impression leads to great reviews.

  • Do: Send a "Post-Stay" message 2-3 hours after checkout. Personalize it with their name.
  • Don't: Pressure them for a 5-star review. The request must be gentle and optional.

💡 Pro Tip: If they left the place tidy, add a personal line like, "We truly appreciate you leaving the space so neat and tidy." It makes them feel valued and more likely to leave a positive review.

Important Notes

On Odor Control (VERY IMPORTANT)

It is critical that the unit has **NO FOUL SMELL** upon entry. Check the sofa, even when closed up. Take extra care if the previous guest was smoking, as the smell clings to linens and fabric. Always use a quality fabric deodorizer and sanitize the entire unit after cleaning is complete and before the next guest arrives.

Safety Tip: Always wear gloves, especially when handling cleaning chemicals. Ensure good ventilation by keeping windows open.

Pro Tip: Use microfiber cloths for a streak-free shine on glass and faucets. Use a separate cloth for the toilet.

⭐ SPOTLESS CLEANING GUIDE: Professional Turnover Checklist NEW

How to Clean Your Airbnb — A Walkthrough for New Airbnb Hosts FEATURED

Follow this top‑to‑bottom, dry‑to‑wet process to achieve “white & spotless” 5‑star standards (kitchen & bathroom focus).

  1. Prep (dry): Open windows; gather caddy; start laundry. Remove trash/linen; pre‑treat tough spots.
  2. Kitchen — top‑to‑bottom: Upper cabinets/hood, lights; wipe backsplash; declutter surfaces. Dry dust → wet degrease → sanitize. Polish stainless; inside microwave; fridge handles & seals.
  3. Kitchen — sink & appliances: Descale faucet; scrub sink (rim/drain); run hot water. Wipe stovetop/knobs; oven door glass; check kettle/coffee maker.
  4. Bathroom — top‑to‑bottom: Vent fan, lights, shelves, mirror. Dust first; then clean tiles, walls.
  5. Bathroom — fixtures (dry→wet): Apply cleaner to bowl, seat hinges, under‑rim; let dwell. Scrub & flush. Descale shower head; squeegee glass; scrub grout/edges.
  6. Floors: Vacuum from far corner → exit; mop last (neutral cleaner). Check behind doors & under vanity/appliances.
  7. Reset & stage: Replace liners; fold towels; restock amenities; set thermostat; photos for QC (“after” angles).
  8. Final inspection: White‑glove test for horizontal surfaces; smell check; run tap/toilet once more; lights off; door/lock test.

Hosting & Operations Essentials

HOUSE OPERATIONS: Complete checklist of maintenance + setup tasks for Airbnbs (or any home) UPDATED

Short, must-know practices to standardize daily hosting work.

  • Quarterly maintenance: Deep-clean AC filters, test smoke/CO alarms, check fire extinguisher gauges, inspect for leaks/mold, and schedule pest control.
  • Monthly quick checks: Verify TV/Wi‑Fi, test major appliances, run hot water for 2–3 minutes per faucet, and confirm spare bulbs/batteries are stocked.
  • Supply tracking: Maintain a par-level sheet (linens, towels, toiletries, coffee/tea, cleaning chemicals). Reorder when stock < 2× turnover sets.
  • Owner closet setup: Lockable cabinet with labeled bins: backup linens, towel sets, paper goods, guest amenities, light bulbs, batteries, filters, tool kit. Post QR to the inventory log.
  • System: One Google Sheet + QR code for cleaners to log usage after each turnover; weekly review + monthly restock run.

Crisis Playbook

Step-by-step responses for every operational emergency.

🚨 A/C or Water Failure

1. Assess: Unit issue or area outage? Confirm immediately.

2. Communicate (in 15 mins): "Hi [Guest Name], I am so sorry for the inconvenience. Our technician is on the way (ETA [Time]) to fix the [A/C/Water]. We'veprovided [a fan/bottled water] for you in the meantime. I will personally monitor this and keep you updated."

3. Act & Follow-Up: Dispatch tech. If not resolved in 3-4 hours, proactively offer a 50% refund for the night and/or relocation assistance.

🚨 Wi-Fi Down

1. Assess: Ask guest to power cycle router (unplug for 60s). Check ISP app for outages.

2. Communicate: "So sorry about the Wi-Fi! Could you please try unplugging the router for one minute? I'm also checking with the provider for any area issues. Will update you in 15 mins."

3. Act: If it persists, offer a mobile hotspot or a refund for a co-working space pass.

🚨 Noise Complaint / Party

1. Communicate Gently: "Hi [Guest Name], just a friendly reminder about our no-party policy and quiet hours after 10 PM to respect our neighbors. Thank you for your cooperation!"

2. Act & Follow-Up: If it persists, give one formal warning. If it continues, terminate the reservation without refund and involve platform support/security. Document all communication with timestamps.

🚨 Smoking Inside

1. Communicate: "Hi [Guest Name], a friendly reminder that our home is strictly smoke-free. The designated smoking area is outside. Thank you for helping us keep the air fresh for all guests."

2. Act: Document evidence. After checkout, charge the disclosed deep-cleaning fee via the Resolution Center.

🚨 Pest Sighting

1. Communicate with Urgency: "Hi [Guest Name], I am mortified to hear this and I sincerely apologize. This is not our standard. I am arranging an immediate professional treatment. Please let me know if you would be comfortable with a deep clean now, or if you would prefer we assist you in finding alternative accommodation with a full refund."

2. Act: Based on the guest's choice, execute flawlessly. Schedule emergency pest control regardless.

🚨 Damage Discovered

1. Act Fast: Take clear photos/videos immediately. Get a quote for repair.

2. Communicate Politely: "Hi [Guest Name], hope you had a safe trip. During cleaning, we noticed the [item] was damaged. I've attached a photo. We can resolve this through the Resolution Center for [cost]. Please let me know if you have any questions."

3. Follow-Up: File the claim via the Resolution Center with all evidence.
